## Listing photos

The five stays and the town of Fernhollow are fictional. The photographs in `web/public/photos/stock/` are free stock interiors from Wikimedia Commons, each released under CC0 1.0 or into the public domain (many are Unsplash uploads from the CC0 era). They do not show any real rental. Each image was resized to 1600px on the long side with metadata stripped; `web/scripts/photo-meta.py` builds the 800px variants and blur-up placeholders.

# 3D model

The nested-stays cutaway on the guest page is built in code with three.js. The floor plan is an invented layout; its adjustable parts are listed in `ASSUMPTIONS` in `web/src/app/ui/nested-stays/plan.ts`. Textures, sky and furniture models are CC0 assets listed in [CREDITS-3D.md](CREDITS-3D.md).
